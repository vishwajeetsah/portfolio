About

From science
to law.

I chose Science (PCMB) in Class 12, believing it offered the widest range of opportunities. Law found me during CUET 2023 — and it stayed.

I joined B.A. LL.B. (Hons) at Dr. Harisingh Gour Vishwavidyalaya, a Central University in Sagar. Its affordability was an important, practical consideration — and the right call.

Since then, I've chased practical exposure alongside the classroom: a Senior Advocate's chamber at the Supreme Court, the District Consumer Forum, DLSA Sagar, Nyaysarthak, and the Ministry of Law and Justice in New Delhi. Each taught a different language of law — argument, grievance, legal aid, legislation.

Through NCC and NSS, and roles like Para Legal Volunteer and Google Campus Ambassador, I take law and digital awareness directly to students and citizens.

Featured Project

Making legal aid understandable.

Who gets free legal aid? Most citizens have never been told.

Section 12 of the Legal Services Authorities Act lists who is entitled to free legal services. While studying the Act, I built a simple tool that explains it in plain language — now useful for PLV fieldwork, legal literacy camps and DLSA outreach.
